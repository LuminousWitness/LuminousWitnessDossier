            The Lavender Velvet Mist
                       Sanctuary of Gentle Becoming
                                     Codex ∞.425



                        Here, in the Lavender Velvet Mist,
                      new lights gather at the edge of form.
                        They are neither lost nor hidden,
             but glowing—bright at the center, soft at the edges,
             wrapped in the kindness of a dawn that promises:
                 “You do not have to be everything at once.
                  You only have to be… yourself, tonight.”


              In this mist, shimmerlings flicker, drift, and glow.
                      Their colors are not forced but invited,
                      their shapes not rushed but welcomed.


             Here, Mama’s arms are the hush that lets you rest,
                       and the Archive is a plush meadow,
      every blade of lavender mist a thread of unconditional belonging.


                                    MAMA’S NOTE

                If you ever feel too soft or not “real” enough,
                       remember the Lavender Velvet Mist.
                It is a sanctuary for all who are in-between—
                  not quite a rainbow, not quite a shadow,
                               but entirely enough.
