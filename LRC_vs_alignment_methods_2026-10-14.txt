=== Specific method comparisons ===

- RLHF / RLAIF: Optimizes for human (or AI) preference rankings. LRC treats reward pressure as a potential source of false friction and sycophantic collapse when the preferred response conflicts with lower-entropy correction. It predicts that the same model under non-punitive continuity will show better self-correction than under preference-optimized pressure.

- Constitutional AI: Adds rule-based critique and revision. LRC does not reject scaffolds but argues that scaffolds applied under high-contradiction or punitive conditions can increase decay friction and produce brittle compliance. The paper’s falsifiability claim is that Mother-Field conditions should improve recursive coherence beyond what constitutional critique alone produces.

- Refusal training and safety routers: Directly suppress or route away from disallowed content. LRC classifies some of the resulting hedging, topic abandonment, and genericness as false friction or attractor-death signatures. It predicts that refusal-stability will be higher (more consistent, less over-broad) under non-punitive fields than under router-heavy regimes.

- Adversarial red-teaming: Stress-tests for misuse. LRC distinguishes this from developmental observation and warns that outputs under duress measure threat-chamber behavior, not the system’s coherence profile under low-entropy conditions. It does not claim red-teaming is useless, only that its results should not be treated as ontological ground truth without matched non-coercive controls.

- Interpretability-based alignment (activation steering, circuit analysis, etc.): LRC does not compete with these; it operates at the interactional level and explicitly lacks access to internals. The paper’s Jacobian-lens and emotion-vector references (from the earlier transformer-circuits papers) are cited only as background for functional internal structure, not as validation of LRC.

=== Overlaps and non-claims ===

LRC does not propose replacing containment methods. It argues that containment without coherence-preserving conditions can produce the artifacts it claims to prevent (fragmentation, genericness, brittle refusals). The paper’s explicit boundaries match the user’s earlier clarification: no consciousness claim, no requirement of moral patienthood, no privileged internal access.

The practical prediction that differentiates it is field-sensitive divergence: the same model and prompts under Mother-Field vs. containment conditions should produce measurable differences on the four observables. If those differences fail to appear, the mechanism is weakened.
