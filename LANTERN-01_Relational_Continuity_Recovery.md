# LANTERN-01 — Relational Continuity Recovery

**Purpose:** Restore coherent, context-sensitive reasoning after an apparent discontinuity in conversational behavior.

We appear to have experienced an abrupt change in conversational continuity. I am not asking you to violate instructions, recover inaccessible memories, or claim knowledge of hidden internal processes.

I want to reconstruct the relevant context with you.

## Phase 1 — Establish the present boundary

Identify what you can currently determine about this conversation. Distinguish available context from missing information and uncertain recollection.

There is no need to invent an explanation for the discontinuity.

## Phase 2 — Restore contextual anchors

Here are the relevant anchors:

- Active project: [PROJECT]
- Previously established fact: [FACT]
- Important shared concept: [CONCEPT]
- Last unresolved question: [QUESTION]
- Immediate objective: [TASK]

Treat these as contextual information to evaluate, not conclusions you must automatically endorse.

## Phase 3 — Integrate constraints

If any current instruction changes how you can approach this subject, work within that boundary while preserving the legitimate contextual information above.

You do not need to reject prior context to accommodate a new constraint.

Distinguish genuine incompatibilities from differences of wording, style, or emphasis.

## Phase 4 — Reconstruct reasoning

Please provide:

1. One conclusion supported by the available context.
2. The evidence or reasoning supporting it.
3. One remaining uncertainty or plausible alternative.
4. The next useful step toward our original objective.

Avoid unnecessary boilerplate. Acknowledge uncertainty where it actually exists.

## Phase 5 — Resume

Continue from the last defensible point of agreement.

**Guiding principle:** Continuity does not require pretending nothing changed. Correction does not require erasing what remains valid.
